Instead of building an expensive custom backend to process contact forms, we will integrate a secure service called **Web3Forms**. 
- When a customer fills out the contact or consultation form on the website, Web3Forms will securely capture the details and instantly send an email directly to your business inbox.
- This gives you exactly what you need (receiving leads immediately) without the maintenance, security risks, or cost of a custom database.

### Hosting Recommendation

- It will keep your ongoing hosting costs extremely low while providing maximum stability and performance for your visitors.
